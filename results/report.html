Assumptions

  • 40-hour weeks, minus regional holidays and PTO. Planning stops at 85% billable; the rest is internal work, enablement, and presales help.
  • Stage weights for the expected case: signed 100%, commit 80%, best case 50%, pipeline 20%.
  • One lead per engagement: someone with room, working the week it kicks off, able to carry at least 90% of it, in the customer's region if it has one, in that order. Overflow goes to others with the skill that week; anything left is unstaffed.
  • New hires ramp at 50% for 6 weeks. A req takes 8 weeks to fill.
  • Demand doesn't pause for holidays, so holiday weeks look tighter than they will be. The trigger rule exists so those weeks never hire anyone on their own.
